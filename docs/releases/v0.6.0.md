# Aries Image Studio 0.6.0

Optional sign-in with Google, so a few invited people can share one Studio with their own
histories, a Grafana dashboard, and phone layout fixes.

## What's new

- **Sign-in with Google, off by default** ([AUTH.md](../AUTH.md)). Only Google accounts on
  an allowlist get in; no passwords. Each account sees only its own jobs, images,
  favorites, reference uploads and preferences; the queue and the cards stay shared, first
  come, first served. The owner inherits everything made before sign-in and is the only
  one who can lend, disable or enable cards.
  - Studio is the OpenID Connect client itself (code flow with PKCE and a nonce).
    Sessions are stored by the hash of their token and renew while used; an address taken
    off the list is signed out on its next request.
  - Signed-in devices keep working without internet, and `aries-image-studio login-link`
    prints a single-use, 10-minute link that signs a device in without Google.
  - Studio can have several public addresses (say an intranet and an internet name); each
    sign-in returns to the one it started on.
  - A sign-in screen when signed out, and the account's picture with a Sign out menu in the
    top bar.
- **Privacy policy and terms** at `/privacy` and `/terms`, public, as Google's consent
  screen requires. They are generated from Studio's settings (the models in use, the cloud
  enhancers switched on, the session length) and speak for whoever hosts Studio.
- **Grafana dashboard** in `deploy/grafana/`: jobs and queue, why tasks wait, each card's
  state and memory, host RAM, stage and model-load times, failures and crashes; the GPU
  host's temperatures, fans and power from node_exporter, drawn against when each card was
  busy; and wall power, energy and time-of-use cost from a metering smart plug.
- **Agent histograms** for stage and model-load times reach 30 minutes; the old buckets
  stopped at 10 s, so their percentiles were meaningless.
- **Phones:** the image page fills the screen on iOS, and scrolling its details gives them
  more room while the image stays in view; queued, running and failed tiles and the
  composer's options fit narrow screens; in the dark theme, no lighter band appears while
  Android Chrome hides its address bar.

## Upgrading from 0.5.0

History, images and preferences carry over; Studio adds its new tables and columns on
start. No setting is required: without the sign-in settings Studio works as before.

- **New settings (Studio):** `ARIES_OIDC_CLIENT_ID`, `ARIES_OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET`,
  `ARIES_AUTH_ALLOWED_EMAILS`, `ARIES_PUBLIC_URL`, `ARIES_AUTH_OWNER`,
  `ARIES_OIDC_ISSUER`, `ARIES_SESSION_DAYS`, `ARIES_LEGAL_OPERATOR`, `ARIES_LEGAL_CONTACT`.
  With the client ID set, Studio does not start without the next three.
- **API:** new `GET /api/me`, `/auth/login`, `/auth/callback`, `/auth/link`,
  `POST /auth/logout`, `GET /healthz`, `/privacy`, `/terms`. With sign-in on, every other
  `/api/*` route needs a session (401 without) and answers for that account only (someone
  else's job or image is 404), writes from another origin are refused, and CORS is
  same-origin.
- **Metrics:** the agent's `aries_task_seconds_bucket` and `aries_worker_load_seconds_bucket`
  series have new bucket bounds; sums and counts are unchanged.

## Known limitations

Sign-in is optional: without it, anyone who can reach Studio's port uses it and sees
everything. Accounts are keyed by email (changing an address leaves its history under the
old one), and there are no per-account quotas. Built and tested only on Intel Arc Pro B60
(XPU), and a stage running when its agent restarts counts as a failed attempt.

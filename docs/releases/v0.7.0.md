# Aries Image Studio 0.7.0

Search by what's in the image, and pictures you can watch forming: a live preview at every
step while an image draws, and a rough one while it waits to be finished.

## What's new

- **Search by what's in the image** ([SEARCH.md](../SEARCH.md)). The gallery's search
  finds images by what they show as well as by the words of their prompts and enhanced
  prompts, and by seed, best first. "Rainy street at night" finds the rainy streets, whatever
  their prompts said.
  - A small badge on each result says what found it: signal bars for how closely its look
    matches (strong, possible, weak), and a mark for the words: in the prompt as written,
    as the exact phrase, or only in the enhanced prompt. Tapping the badge spells it out.
    Results found only by a faint resemblance come last, under **Weaker matches**.
  - **More like this**, on a tile and in the viewer, ranks the gallery by how alike the
    images look to one.
  - It all runs in Studio, on its CPU, with a SigLIP 2 model built into the Studio image: it
    works while the GPU host sleeps or its cards are busy, and nothing leaves Studio. A
    search answers in tens of milliseconds.
- **Live preview while drawing.** The tile shows the picture forming at every denoise step,
  with progress and time left.
- **Preview before decode.** Once an image is drawn, its tile shows a rough preview while
  the decode waits for a card (often a minute or more in a batch) and while it runs. Studio
  makes it without a model or a GPU.
- **Enhancer names:** the enhancer that runs on the cards is called Local (was Built-in),
  and while a cloud enhancer rewrites a prompt, the card names its model.
- **Fix:** step previews of full-size images no longer cut the worker off from its agent
  (the worker then kept denoising for nobody); a worker whose agent goes away now stops its
  job and exits.

## Upgrading from 0.6.0

History, images and preferences carry over. Update both images: live previews and the fix
need the new agent.

- **Studio** adds its search tables on start, then indexes every existing image in the
  background, newest first; until an image is indexed, search finds it by its words.
- **Size:** the Studio image is about 830 MB compressed (was about 165 MB), and Studio uses
  about 1.2 GB of RAM, most of it the search model.
- **New settings (Studio):** `ARIES_SEARCH_MODEL_DIR` (set in the image),
  `ARIES_SEARCH_THREADS`, `ARIES_PREVIEW_EVERY_STEPS`, `ARIES_PREVIEW_HALVE_ABOVE_PX`.
  None is required.
- **API:** new `GET /api/jobs?like=<id>` and `GET /api/jobs/{id}/preview`. With `q` or
  `like`, `GET /api/jobs` pages are ranked, with offset cursors, and each job carries
  `search_match`, and as they apply `search_strength`, `search_words` and `search_phrase`.
  Jobs that are drawing carry `preview_step`.
- **Protocol:** `DenoiseParams` gains `preview_every` and `preview_downscale`, and
  `Progress` a `preview` JPEG.
- **Metrics:** new `aries_search_embed_seconds`, `aries_search_indexed_images`,
  `aries_preview_seconds`, `aries_step_preview_seconds` and `aries_step_preview_bytes`; the
  Grafana dashboard has panels for them.
- **Building from source:** Studio needs Rust 1.92.

## Known limitations

Search's thresholds and strength levels were tuned on a small set of images, and its word
search skips English filler words only. Sign-in is optional: without it, anyone who can
reach Studio's port uses it and sees everything. Built and tested only on Intel Arc Pro B60
(XPU), and a stage running when its agent restarts counts as a failed attempt.

# Aries Image Studio 0.8.0

Studio opens up to other tools and to visitors: AI tools and scripts can make and find images
with an access token, and an optional read-only demo shows anyone the images you choose.

## What's new

- **CLI and AI tools** ([INTEGRATIONS.md](../INTEGRATIONS.md)).
  - **Access tokens:** a **Connections** page, in the account menu, makes and revokes tokens that
    let a tool act as your account. Each is shown once, Studio keeps only its hash, and the page
    shows when it was last used.
  - **MCP server** at `/mcp`, for AI tools such as Antigravity: `generate_images`, `get_jobs`
    (waits for jobs and returns the finished images as pictures), `find_images` (search and more
    like this), `view_image`, `set_favorite`, `cancel_jobs` and `studio_status`.
  - **`aries` command line:** one Python file with no dependencies. Make images and save the
    PNGs, wait, show, download, search, upload reference images, favorite, cancel, and see status.
  - **Skill** for AI tools (`skills/aries-studio-mcp`): picking sizes and aspect ratios, waiting
    for jobs, and showing the results with their Studio links.
- **Read-only demo** ([DEMO.md](../DEMO.md)), off unless `ARIES_DEMO=1`, with sign-in on.
  - The sign-in screen gains **Open guest demo**. The click accepts the terms and privacy policy,
    and gives the browser a new random id. Without one, nothing is shown.
  - Guests browse the timeline, gallery, viewer, search and a reduced status page, over the images
    you marked only, with their prompts and references. The composer can be tried, but nothing
    can be sent or changed: Studio refuses it, whatever the page does.
  - What guests are sent is named field by field, and leaves out hosts, favorites and image
    metadata. Limits apply per address.
  - The owner marks images and whole groups with a globe button, confirming each time, and reviews
    them with an **In demo** filter. Sharing an image made from uploaded pictures is not
    recommended: it needs an explicit warning accepted, which shows the uploads and the risks.
  - `/privacy` and `/terms` describe the demo when it is on. `/metrics` counts unique visitors,
    requests and refusals, and the Grafana dashboard has a **Public demo** row.
- **Link previews and search engines** ([SEO.md](../SEO.md)). Shared links show a card with a
  collage of generated images. With a demo, `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` list the public pages;
  without one, Studio asks not to be crawled.

- **Security fixes.**
  - Only the browser that started a sign-in can finish it. A link holding someone else's sign-in
    could put another invited person in that account, where what they made next was readable.
  - The address to return to after signing in could lead to another site, with a tab in it.
  - Another site's page can no longer sign you out.

## Upgrading from 0.7.0

History, images and preferences carry over, and Studio adds its new tables on start. Only Studio
changed: the agent is the same but for its version number, so updating it is optional. A sign-in in
progress during the update has to start again.

- **New settings (Studio):** `ARIES_DEMO`, `ARIES_DEMO_TRUSTED_PROXIES`,
  `ARIES_DEMO_REQUESTS_PER_MIN`, `ARIES_DEMO_SEARCHES_PER_MIN`, `ARIES_DEMO_DOWNLOADS_PER_MIN` and
  `ARIES_DEMO_GUESTS_PER_MIN`. None is required. To use the demo, update your copy of
  `deploy/studio/compose.yaml`, which passes them on.
- **Behind a proxy,** set `ARIES_DEMO_TRUSTED_PROXIES` to how many there are, or all visitors share
  one address and its limits.
- **Link previews** use `ARIES_PUBLIC_URL`, so they need sign-in on. Your proxy must let crawlers
  reach `/`, `/og-image.jpg`, `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/terms` and `/privacy`.
- **API:** new `GET/POST /api/tokens`, `DELETE /api/tokens/{id}`, `POST /mcp`,
  `GET /api/jobs/{id}/uploads`, `POST /auth/guest`, `GET /robots.txt` and `GET /sitemap.xml`.
  `PATCH /api/jobs/{id}` takes `demo` and `accept_reference_risk`, and `GET /api/jobs` takes `demo`
  and `batch`. With a token, `GET /api/me` says whose it is; for a guest it says `visitor`.
- **Metrics:** new `aries_demo_unique_visitors`, `aries_demo_guests_total`,
  `aries_demo_requests_total` and `aries_demo_refused_total`.

## Known limitations

Sign-in is optional: without it, anyone who can reach Studio's port uses it and sees everything, and
so can any web page opened on that network. Agent traffic, including the shared token, is not
encrypted. The demo's visitor count is an estimate, starts over with Studio, and counts someone who clears their
cookies again. Its terms have no version, so a guest is not asked again when they change until their
id runs out, within 30 days. Built and tested only on Intel Arc Pro B60 (XPU), and a stage running
when its agent restarts counts as a failed attempt.
